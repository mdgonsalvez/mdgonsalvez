Common questions

  • Does it cost anything? No — Passport Quest is free, with no in-app purchases.
  • Does it need the internet? No. The whole game works offline and all progress is saved on your device.
  • Are there ads or tracking? None. No ads, no analytics, no accounts.
  • How do I reset progress? Open Settings in the app → Reset all progress.
