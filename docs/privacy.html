Passport Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Passport Quest is built for children and designed to be completely private and safe.

Data we collect

None. Passport Quest does not collect, store, or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to identify you.

Your game progress

Your stamps, Hint Coins and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Internet & tracking

The app makes no network connections. There are no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Purchases & links

There are no in-app purchases and no links that take a child out of the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data and has no network features, it complies with children's privacy regulations such as COPPA and GDPR-K.
